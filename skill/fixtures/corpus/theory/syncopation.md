# Syncopation — technical fixture

Original explanatory text for testing retrieval, not an imported theory source.

## Meter, accents, and grouping

Syncopation involves accents or attacks that conflict with an established metrical expectation. A grouping of 3 + 3 + 2 eighth-note durations totals eight eighth notes. The grouping alone does not describe every musical context or determine the phase of an algorithmic rhythm.

## Choosing an example

Compare a steady beat with displaced accents. Listen in the actual music environment before making claims about the result; the RAG system does not render or listen to audio.
